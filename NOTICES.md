## Ported code

Zic's protocol stack (`lib/protocol/`, `lib/state/`, `lib/notifications/`,
most of `lib/ui/`), the home-screen widget, and the associated test suite
are ported from **[ZLinker](https://github.com/opensymph/ZLinker)** (MIT,
Copyright (c) 2026 ZRemote contributors). Those files keep their MIT
license; this notice satisfies the license's attribution condition.

The v4 login module (`lib/auth/`), the Zic design system
(`lib/ui/theme.dart`, `lib/design/`), and the brand assets are original
to this project. The repository root stays MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Zic
contributors. The in-app rights statement reserves the Zic name, the Zic
mark, and distribution of the install package. It does not cancel the MIT
